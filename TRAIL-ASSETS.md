# Houston trail preview assets

The eight files in `preview-src/public/media/trails/` are unchanged copies of the Android app's approved November 23, 2026 trail collection. They contain public park imagery and static area maps, not private location or recorded-run data. Photo/source/license links are also available in each preview trail detail.

- `trail_memorial_photo.jpg`: Beverly Moseley / USDA NRCS Texas, [original photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Joggers_along_the_Seymour_Lieberman_Exer-Trail_at_Memorial_Park_in_Houston.NRCS_photo_by_Beverly_Moseley._(25116673655).jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) (also identified by the source as US federal public-domain work).
- `trail_hermann_photo.jpg`: WhisperToMe, [Marvin Taylor Trail](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MarvinTaylorTrailHoustonTX.JPG), public domain.
- `trail_buffalo_bayou_photo.jpg`: Michael Barera, [Buffalo Bayou Park and skyline](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Houston_May_2022_13_(Buffalo_Bayou_Park_and_skyline).jpg), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The image and its display crop remain under that license.
- `trail_terry_hershey_photo.jpg`: Scott Ehardt, [Terry Hershey Park trail](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Terry_Hershey_Park_trail.jpg), public domain.

Photographs were resized by their source; CSS crops their display to fit. No content edits were made.

The four `trail_*_map.png` files are original RunState cartography rendered from unmodified OpenStreetMap vector extracts on November 23, 2026. Map data © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), Open Database License 1.0. These are park-area overviews, not recorded routes or navigation. No hosted OpenStreetMap raster tiles are bundled.

No live map, location permission, route recording, weather, trail status or Spotify service is requested by this preview.
